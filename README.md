# Decagon Pharma Hub

Create a premium, modern, professional pharmaceutical company website for:

DECAGON

Tagline: “Believed Quality”

DECAGON is a pharmaceutical company with a diverse portfolio of tablets, capsules, syrups, injections, creams, shampoos, soaps, supplements, and healthcare products.

IMPORTANT:

Use the uploaded DECAGON logo as the primary brand identity.

Do NOT redesign or alter the logo.

Use the exact logo proportions and visual identity throughout the website.

1. BRAND IDENTITY & COLOR PALETTE

==================================================

Build the complete UI around the DECAGON logo colors.

Primary Brand Colors extracted from the logo:

- Deep Royal Blue: #303F8F

- Purple: #A844C7

- Teal: #4CB5AD

- Golden Yellow: #FFD000

- Orange: #F58220

- White: #FFFFFF

- Dark Text: #252525

- Light Background: #F7F9FC

Use:

- Royal Blue for primary buttons, headings and important navigation elements

- Purple for secondary highlights and gradients

- Teal for healthcare/medical highlights

- Golden Yellow and Orange as accent colors

- White for clean pharmaceutical sections

- Very light blue/grey backgrounds for alternate sections

Use subtle brand gradients such as:

Royal Blue → Purple

Teal → Royal Blue

Yellow → Orange

Do NOT overuse gradients.

The website must feel premium, trustworthy and pharmaceutical rather than flashy.
